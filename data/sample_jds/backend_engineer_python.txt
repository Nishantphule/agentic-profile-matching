Senior Backend Engineer (Python)

About the role
We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to build the next generation of our payments platform. You will design distributed systems that handle millions of transactions a day, with strong reliability and security guarantees.

Responsibilities
- Design and implement scalable microservices in Python (FastAPI / Django).
- Own service reliability: monitoring, alerting, on-call.
- Partner with data engineering on event-driven pipelines (Kafka).
- Mentor mid-level engineers and lead design reviews.

Required qualifications
- 4+ years of professional Python backend experience.
- Production experience with FastAPI or Django.
- Strong SQL skills (Postgres) and experience designing schemas.
- Experience deploying services on AWS or GCP.
- Hands-on Docker and Kubernetes experience.
- Familiar with REST API design and observability.

Preferred / nice to have
- Kafka or similar streaming systems.
- Experience with Terraform.
- AWS certification (Solutions Architect or DevOps Engineer).
- gRPC and GraphQL experience.
- Open-source contributions.
