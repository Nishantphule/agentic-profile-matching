Machine Learning Platform Engineer

About the role
Join our ML Platform team to build the infrastructure that powers all production ML at the company - feature stores, training pipelines, model registries, and online inference services. This is a senior IC role with significant scope and autonomy.

Responsibilities
- Build and operate large-scale data and ML pipelines on AWS.
- Own the model deployment story: CI/CD for models, A/B testing, monitoring.
- Partner with applied ML teams to productionise PyTorch / scikit-learn models.
- Lead architecture for the next-generation feature store.

Required qualifications
- 4+ years of backend / platform engineering experience.
- Strong Python skills.
- Hands-on AWS experience (S3, EKS, SageMaker, Lambda).
- Proven track record with Docker and Kubernetes in production.
- Experience with Airflow, Spark, or a comparable orchestration / data-processing stack.
- Experience deploying ML models to production.

Preferred / nice to have
- Experience with LangChain, RAG, or LLM inference at scale.
- Familiarity with Terraform and infrastructure-as-code.
- Kafka or other streaming experience.
- Snowflake or BigQuery experience.
- Open-source contributions to ML / data tooling.
